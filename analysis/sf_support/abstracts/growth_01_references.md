_Rationale:_ The corpus contains numerous empirical studies (fish, bivalves, bears) in which the von Bertalanffy growth model successfully describes length-at-age, directly corroborating the stylized fact that isomorphic organisms follow vBGF growth. Several theoretical papers (8, 9, 16) further show that optimal resource allocation produces curves resembling von Bertalanffy trajectories, reinforcing the pattern's generality. The main caveats are that the empirical studies often do not explicitly verify 'abundant food' conditions, and snippet 17 notes that some short-lived gobies show little or no asymptotic phase and are sometimes better fit by a Broken Stick model, indicating exceptions. Overall the evidence is strong and predominantly supportive, though strictly the literature concerns descriptive fit rather than the DEB-specific mechanistic conditions of isomorphy and constant abundant food.
