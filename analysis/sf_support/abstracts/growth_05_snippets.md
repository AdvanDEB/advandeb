**Stylized fact:** The inverse of the von Bertalanffy growth rate for organisms of the same species at different food availabilities decreases linearly with ultimate length.

> Bertalanffy's equation is commonly used to model indeterminate growth. Bertalanffy claimed that this growth pattern results from growth potential decreasing with age.
