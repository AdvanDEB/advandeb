_Rationale:_ The stylized fact concerns weak homeostasis: constant chemical composition at constant food density. Several snippets bear on organismal stoichiometric homeostasis. Snippet 15 most directly supports it, showing herbivorous invertebrates maintain narrow C:P/C:N ratios despite variable food, indicating homeostatic regulation. Snippets 2, 6, 11, and 17 support the concept of compositional/elemental homeostasis, though they emphasize that the degree of homeostasis varies with growth rate and energy availability. Conversely, snippet 1 explicitly contradicts strict elemental homeostasis in a copepod, proposing 'rheostasis' instead. Importantly, none of these snippets test the precise DEB condition of constant food density driving convergence to constant composition—they mostly address responses to variable or imbalanced food. The remaining snippets are off-topic. Overall there is moderate, somewhat mixed support: homeostasis is broadly observed but its strictness is debated, and direct evidence for the constant-food-density convergence claim is sparse, so consensus is best rated as insufficient/neutral leaning supportive.
