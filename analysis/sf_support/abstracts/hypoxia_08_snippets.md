# Evidence snippets — hypoxia_08

**Scope:** Reproduction abstracts  
**Stylized fact:** O2 consumption starts to diminish at a reduced PO2 below a critical saturation level; when an animal has an elevated O2 consumption (swimming, digesting, etc.), consumption starts to diminish at a greater PO2 than the critical saturation.
