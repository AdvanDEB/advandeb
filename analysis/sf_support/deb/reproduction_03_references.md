Snippets [9] and [17] (Sara & van der Meer) describe model populations stabilizing in a state where 'spawning events do not occur anymore' and individuals remain immature under low food while still surviving, corroborating the existence of a survive-but-no-reproduction regime. Snippet [5] similarly describes food levels too low for reproduction yet some individuals persisting. Snippet [13] shows reproduction continuing during weight loss/starvation, which mildly cuts against an absolute no-reproduction threshold but concerns decreasing rather than constant low food. Most other snippets are topically related (starvation, food thresholds, maintenance) but do not directly bear on whether a constant low-food regime permits survival yet never initiates reproduction. Overall the corpus offers moderate, consistent support with little genuine conflict, though much of it is theoretical/model-based rather than empirical.
