This is a direct contradiction of the stylized fact as literally stated. The supporting snippets ([2], [3], [4], [6], [11]) all originate from the Mauritsson/Maur applied PCB-modelling work, which adopts exponential foetal growth as a simplifying assumption (citing Peters 1983), not as a validated empirical finding within DEB theory. Thus, while several snippets nominally 'support' exponential growth, they reflect a modelling convenience rather than corroborating evidence, whereas the foundational DEB literature explicitly favors the cubed-time (Huggett-Widdas) relation. The weight of authoritative DEB evidence therefore leans against the exact stylized fact, yielding a predominantly contradicted/mixed direction.
