_Rationale:_ Multiple snippets directly corroborate the stylized fact. Snippet [8] explicitly states the fact verbatim, and snippets [2], [5], [10], [12], and [14] describe the hyperbolic (type II) functional response in which ingestion/feeding rate saturates to a maximum at high food density and becomes effectively independent of food concentration (e.g., the incipient limiting level in [2], the >80% saturation threshold in [12]). Snippets [3], [7], [9], [13] support the related scaled functional response framework where f approaches 1 at abundant food. The only contradicting note is [18], which discusses social/ratio-dependent feeding effects making feeding rate not solely a function of food density, but this is presented as an open extension rather than a refutation of the saturation principle. Overall the corpus strongly and consistently supports the fact.
