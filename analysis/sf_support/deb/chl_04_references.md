_Rationale:_ Multiple snippets, especially from LoreMarq2010, explicitly state the stylized fact (SF8) that chlorophyll concentration decreases with irradiance and use it to constrain model structure (snippets 3, 4, 8, 13). Kooy2010 (5) and Zonn98b (10) corroborate that relative chlorophyll is high at low irradiance, consistent with the fact. The main tension comes from Zonneveld (6, 14), which note that while chlorophyll production rate decreases with decreasing irradiance, the chlorophyll cell quota still increases at low light due to reduced dilution by growth—an alternative mechanistic framing that nonetheless ends up consistent with higher chlorophyll at low irradiance (i.e., decreasing with irradiance). These were labeled 'contradicts' because they challenge the simplistic production-based interpretation, but directionally they align. Overall the corpus provides strong, repeated support for the fact with little genuine conflict.
