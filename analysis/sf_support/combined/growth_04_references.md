_Rationale:_ Several snippets directly bear on the stylized fact and support it. Snippet 3 is a verbatim statement of the fact citing Kooijman 2000 across bacteria, yeasts, and animals. Snippets 1, 10, and 11 (Teix2015, Kooy88c) provide log-log plots showing the von Bertalanffy growth rate decreasing with maximum size/length corrected for a common temperature, consistent with a slope near -1 driven by reserve scaling. Snippet 5 (CardVeer2006) confirms the temperature-corrected growth rate scaled inversely with maximum length with a slope close to -1. Snippet 2 attributes the variation to temperature and size, consistent with the mechanism. The main partial counter is snippet 12, which found the relationship negative but not significant when no temperature correction was applied — but the same paper (snippet 5) shows that after temperature correction the expected near -1 slope emerges, reinforcing rather than refuting the fact. The many other snippets concern the temperature-size rule or species-specific growth fits and are off-topic. Overall the directly relevant DEB-corpus evidence is multiple and clearly corroborating.
