_Rationale:_ Multiple snippets directly affirm that survivor curves for aging-terminated life span are well described by the Weibull and Gompertz models. The DEB sources (Kooijman, LeeuVera2010, KooyHoev89, Kooy93) explicitly derive how the DEB ageing module reduces to the Weibull model (shape parameter 3) or the general Gompertz model and note these are the two famous, frequently used aging models with good empirical fit (snippets 1,2,3,6,7,8,9). Independent literature corroborates these models describing senescence in birds, mammals, and plants (13,15,16,20,22). Only snippet 14 offers a caveat—Weibull can be inaccurate when mortality is concentrated in specific life stages—but this qualifies rather than overturns the general fact. Overall the evidence is strong and consistent, supporting the stylized fact.
