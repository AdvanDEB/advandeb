_Rationale:_ Multiple DEB-theory sources (snippets 1-7, 9-11) explicitly state the stylized fact that dioxygen use scales differently across developmental stages: increasing with decreasing mass in embryos but increasing with mass in juveniles and adults. This directly affirms that intraspecific scaling of maximum dioxygen use changes with developmental stage. Snippet 1 gives the mechanistic rationale (reserve loss during embryo development vs. structure-driven maintenance in juveniles/adults). Empirical studies (19, 22) independently document stage-dependent metabolic scaling and developmental-stage differences in respiration rate in silkworm and fish, corroborating the pattern. The remaining snippets concern oxygen/redox effects on development or unrelated topics and are neutral. No snippet contradicts the fact, so the evidence is strong and consistent.
