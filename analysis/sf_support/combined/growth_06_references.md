_Rationale:_ Multiple DEB-theory sources explicitly state and validate that foetal weight increases approximately proportional to cubed time, deriving this from the model assumption that reserve transfer from mother to foetus scales with surface area, yielding dL/dt = v/3 and V(t) proportional to (vt/3)^3 (snippets 2, 3, 9, 10), with snippet 1 noting an excellent fit to empirical data from Huggett and Widdas (1951) and snippet 8 corroborating analogous cubic-time growth in pupae. These are clear, repeated supporting references with strong relevance. Some clinical/physiological snippets describe fetal growth as exponential in mid-gestation or linear in late gestation (13, 16), which superficially conflict, but the DEB model itself notes the curve resembles but is less steep than exponential; these contradictions are weaker and context-dependent. The broader perinatal/twin-pregnancy snippets are largely off-topic. Overall the corpus strongly corroborates the stylized fact.
