_Rationale:_ The stylized fact—that some energy always dissipates, even in the absence of dioxygen—is well supported across the corpus. The strongest direct evidence comes from Kooy2010_c and Kooy2013, which explicitly state that life exists in anaerobic environments, so dioxygen use (respiration) cannot serve as a universal quantifier of metabolic rate, and that entropy/heat dissipation must be used instead; some microbes even absorb heat rather than produce it, underscoring that dissipation (entropy production) is the universal invariant. BallMart2018 states dissipation is 'always present' due to thermodynamic inefficiency, and YenPaga2014 asserts 'living organisms always dissipate energy.' The JusuKear papers frame the organism as a thermodynamic system that necessarily dissipates. Numerous origin-of-life and thermodynamics snippets reinforce that living/dissipative systems require continuous free-energy dissipation. No snippet contradicts the fact. Evidence is strong and consensus is clearly corroborating.
