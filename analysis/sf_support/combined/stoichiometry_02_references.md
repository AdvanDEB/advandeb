_Rationale:_ Multiple core DEB references (snippets 1, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11) explicitly define and assert the weak homeostasis assumption: that organisms at constant food density converge to constant chemical composition during growth, with several noting this matches empirical patterns on constant stoichiometry. These provide clear, direct corroboration. Some supporting evidence comes with the nuance that constancy occurs after an adaptation period and depends on sufficient energy availability (snippet 14, 23). One snippet (13) contradicts strict homeostasis by reporting intrastage changes in carbon/nitrogen content in a copepod, proposing 'rheostasis' instead. However, this is a single empirical exception against a strong body of theoretical and empirical support, and DEB itself frames weak homeostasis as applying after adaptation at truly constant conditions. The remaining snippets are off-topic or only tangentially related to general homeostasis. Overall the corpus strongly corroborates the stylized fact.
