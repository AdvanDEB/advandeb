_Rationale:_ Several snippets directly affirm the stylized fact: snippet 8 states it almost verbatim ('At abundant food, the feeding rate is at some maximum, independent of food density'), and snippets 2, 5, 7, 10, and 12 describe the hyperbolic/saturating functional response in which ingestion increases with food density up to an incipient limiting level and then becomes constant at saturation. Snippet 12 explicitly explains that above ~4x the saturation constant intake is practically indistinguishable from the constant maximum, and snippets 3 and 9 invoke f=1 (scaled functional response of one) at ad libitum food. No snippets contradict the fact; the many density-dependence/ecology snippets (4, 16-24) are off-topic and address population regulation rather than the individual feeding-rate saturation. The evidence is strong and consistent.
