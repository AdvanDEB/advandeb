_Rationale:_ The corpus provides strong and consistent support for the stylized fact that organisms can grow, reproduce, and survive for some time during starvation. Multiple snippets describe organisms enduring transient low-food periods by mobilizing reserves (1, 3, 5, 8), with growth continuing in the early part of starvation (2) and reproduction persisting (4, 14, 17). Numerous references document measurable starvation times/resistance across taxa (rotifers, Drosophila, C. elegans, insects) (15, 17, 18, 20, 22, 23), and physiological mechanisms such as reduced metabolic rate and lipid catabolism enabling survival (16). DEB-framework snippets explicitly model survival and reproduction under variable/low food, with death only when reserves cannot meet maintenance (5, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12). No snippet contradicts the fact; a few are merely topical (13, 19, 21, 24). Overall the evidence is corroborative with little conflict.
