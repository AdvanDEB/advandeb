_Rationale:_ Several DEB-relevant snippets bear on the fact that simpler/passive transport systems incur lower maintenance costs. Snippet 8 is the most direct: Krogh's calculation that simple passive diffusion suffices for small organisms' maintenance, with active processes only needed at larger sizes, directly supports the idea that passive transport is the cheaper, lower-maintenance option. Snippets 1, 5, 9, and 11 from Wier2005 reinforce this by framing active transport as a costly investment adopted only when diffusion becomes inadequate (i.e., implying passive/simpler transport is cheaper to maintain).
