_Rationale:_ The stylized fact as stated claims the inverse von Bertalanffy growth rate DECREASES linearly with ultimate length. The DEB literature consistently states the OPPOSITE: the inverse von Bertalanffy growth rate INCREASES linearly with ultimate length (snippets 1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 11), and equivalently the growth coefficient increases with decreasing food / smaller ultimate size. The fact statement appears to invert the established direction, so the corpus predominantly contradicts the fact as literally stated. Evidence is moderately strong but points against the stated direction.
