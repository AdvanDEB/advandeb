**Scope:** Combined (both corpora)

**Stylized fact:** Above the critical saturation, compensatory mechanisms compensate the O2 reduction to maintain respiration and sustain the standard metabolic rate. These responses can lead to increased O2 consumption (e.g. ventilation rate, heartbeat, mobility).
