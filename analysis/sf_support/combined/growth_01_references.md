_Rationale:_ Multiple core DEB sources (Kooijman 2010, 2012, 1993; Sousa & Domingos; Lika & Kooijman; Karkach) explicitly state that growth of isomorphic organisms at abundant food is well described by the von Bertalanffy growth curve, with one noting an 'amazingly large fraction' of field-collected datasets fit the curve across 261 species. Numerous empirical fisheries and biology studies fit von Bertalanffy curves to diverse species (fish, brown bear), corroborating the pattern. A few snippets debate the mechanistic origin (optimal allocation vs. decreasing growth potential) but do not contradict the empirical adequacy of the curve at abundant food; they affirm that growth curves closely resemble von Bertalanffy's. Evidence is strong and consensus clearly supportive.
